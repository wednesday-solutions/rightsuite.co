# Ad Creative Testing Tool: What to Look For Before You Spend

A good pre-spend ad creative testing tool shows your ad to people who match your real audience, on the platform and format it'll run on, and tells you why they'd stop, click or scroll past. Look for a separate hook score, objections in buyers' own words and concrete rewrites. Then put live budget behind only the creative that survives.

## What to look for in an ad creative testing tool

| Look for | Why it matters | Red flag |
|---|---|---|
| Your real audience | A hook that stops a founder may bore a marketing manager | Generic "consumer panel" you can't describe |
| Platform and format context | A LinkedIn feed ad and a TikTok video are read very differently | One score for every platform |
| Hook scored on its own | The hook decides whether anything else gets seen | Only an overall score |
| Reasons in buyers' words | You need to know what to change | Numbers with no explanation |
| Objections and rewrites | Turns a score into a next draft | No suggestions |
| Fast, cheap reruns | You'll iterate several times | Each test costs as much as a live round |
| Honest about limits | A simulation isn't a live result | Promises of guaranteed CTR |

## Pre-spend testing vs A/B testing on live spend

Both have a place. Here's how they compare.

| | Pre-spend testing (RightAd) | Live A/B testing |
|---|---|---|
| What it measures | Simulated buyers' reactions and reasons | Real impressions, clicks and conversions |
| Cost per round | Credits, no media spend | Media budget for every variant |
| Time per round | Minutes | Days, until each variant gets enough delivery |
| Tells you why | Yes: objections, takeaways, rewrites | Rarely; you see what happened, not why |
| Works on small budgets | Yes | Often can't reach a clear result |
| Ground truth | No, it's a first read | Yes, for that audience and period |

The practical order: test pre-spend to cut weak creative and fix the obvious problems, then run a live test between the best two. You spend live budget only on creative that's already passed a first filter.

## Symptom, check, fix

| Symptom | Check | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Low scroll-stop rate | Hook score and top objection | Rewrite the hook around one specific pain the audience recognises |
| Stops the scroll, low click likelihood | Clarity and relevance scores | Make the offer and next step obvious; match the CTA to the objective |
| Low credibility | Objections mention "too good to be true" | Swap claims for something you can show: a product shot, a real example |
| Poor platform fit | Format and tone for the placement | Rework for the platform: shorter for feeds, native-looking for TikTok |
| High fatigue risk | How much the ad leans on one surprise | Prepare a second variant before you scale |
